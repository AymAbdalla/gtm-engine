# Ledgerline ICP (made-up example)

Ledgerline sells spend management software to finance teams. Everything on this page is invented to show the shape of an ICP page.

## Who we sell to

- Companies with 200 to 2,000 employees. Sweet spot: 300 to 1,000, where most won deals sit. *(evidence: won-deals list)*
- In the US and Canada. *(judgment: support hours cover these time zones)*
- Industries: logistics, manufacturing, professional services, healthcare services, software. *(evidence: customer list)*

## Who we do not sell to

- Government, education and nonprofit. *(judgment: long buying cycles, procurement rules)*
- Competitors, current customers and partners.
- Companies outside the size band. Companies with unknown headcount get enriched before anyone decides.

## Personas

| Persona | Lane | Role | Why they matter |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFO / VP Finance | finance | decision maker | Owns the budget and the close |
| Controller | finance | champion | Feels the month-end pain first |
| AP Manager | finance | influencer | Runs the daily process the product changes |
| IT Director | tech | technical gatekeeper | Approves systems and access |
| Procurement lead | ops | influencer | Runs vendor review |

Not buyers: interns, students, recruiters, sales reps. Review only when no persona matched: consultants, advisors, founders.
