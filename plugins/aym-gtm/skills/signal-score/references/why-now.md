# Writing why-now lines

A why-now line is one sentence that says what happened and when, using only what the source says. It is the reason a busy person might read your message. If the line is wrong, the message is wrong.

## Rules

1. One fact, from the source, with the source's date.
2. No guesses about what the fact means for them. Save the angle for the message.
3. No numbers the source did not state.
4. No intent or research-interest data, ever. Use it to pick timing only.
5. For a leader gap, never name or contact the person who left as the target.
6. If there is nothing dated to point to, say "weak case" and name what to watch for. Do not invent urgency.
7. A person must open the link before the first touch. If the page is gone or says something else, drop the line.

## Good and bad examples (all made up)

| Source says | Bad line | Good line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Press release, 2026-09-28: Brightmill Foods names Dana Whitfield CFO. | Brightmill's new CFO is overhauling finance and needs better spend controls. | Named Dana Whitfield as Chief Financial Officer (source dated 2026-09-28). |
| Job post, 2026-10-05: Northpine Logistics is hiring an Accounts Payable Manager. | Northpine is drowning in invoices. | Posted an opening for an Accounts Payable Manager (source dated 2026-10-05). |
| News, 2026-06-30: Silverbirch Clinics raised a growth round to open four clinics. | Silverbirch just raised millions and is ready to buy. | Closed a growth funding round to open four new clinics (source dated 2026-06-30). |
| Intent report, 2026-10-02: research surge on AP automation at Ridgeway. | Saw your team has been researching AP automation. | Timing only: research interest seen 2026-10-02. Use as timing only, do not mention it. |
| Nothing current at Fernhollow, only a layoff notice. | Reaching out since you are restructuring. | Weak case: nothing dated to point to. Watch for a new finance leader. |

## From line to message

The why-now line is not the message. In the message, the fact becomes a short, natural opener, and the rest of the message asks one question. outreach-draft checks the copy. signal-score only makes sure the fact underneath it is true, dated and fresh.
